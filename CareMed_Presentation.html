CareMed

A smart app that helps Alzheimer's patients remember their medication and tracks how they respond.

The Problem

Alzheimer's patients may struggle to:

  • Remember when to take their medication.
  • Remember whether they already took it.
  • Follow instructions in order, such as eating before taking a pill.
  • Use complicated mobile apps.

Meanwhile, the family may not find out in time whether the patient took the medication.

The Solution

CareMed is a simple app that works with the patient through alerts and voice interaction instead of relying on screen use.

  • Reminds the patient when a medication is due.
  • Explains what to do before taking it.
  • Asks by voice whether the step was done.
  • Checks the patient's response.
  • Alerts the caregiver if there is no response.

Who Uses the App?

The caregiver

Enters the patient's details, medications, schedules, and a contact number.

The patient

Receives alerts and responds by voice, with no need to navigate the phone in a complicated way.

Setting Up the Patient Account

To begin, the caregiver enters:

  • The patient's name.
  • The caregiver's contact number.
  • The name of each medication.
  • The time each medication is due.
  • Whether the patient must eat before taking it.
  • How long to wait for the patient's response.

Once saved, the app starts monitoring automatically.

How the App Works

  1. The medication time arrives.
  2. The app sends the patient a voice alert.
  3. If the medication needs food, it asks the patient to eat first.
  4. It asks: "Did you eat your meal? Say yes or no."
  5. Once confirmed, it tells the patient it is time for the medication.
  6. It asks: "Did you take the medication? Say yes or no."
  7. It records the result.

Voice Interaction

The patient does not have to search inside the app or deal with many menus.

App"Did you take your blood pressure medication? Say yes or no."
Patient"Yes."
AppRecords that the dose was confirmed.

If the patient says something else: "Please answer with one of two words: yes or no."

If the Patient Says No

  • The app does not record the dose as taken.
  • It reminds the patient to take the medication.
  • It checks again after the set waiting time.

The goal is to give the patient a chance to respond, rather than treating the dose as missed right away.

If the Patient Does Not Respond

The app repeats the alert or question, based on the settings.

If the set time passes with no response, the app sends an alert to the caregiver.

Example: "Blood pressure medication was not confirmed at the scheduled time."

A Complete Scenario

2:00 PM. Medication time.

App"It is time to eat your meal before your medication."
App"Did you eat your meal? Say yes or no."
Patient"Yes."
App"It is now time for your blood pressure medication. Did you take it? Say yes or no."
Patient"Yes."

The dose was recorded successfully.

What Happens When There Is a Problem?

Patient says "no"
The patient is reminded and followed up.
Anything other than yes or no
The app asks for one of the two words.
No reply
The alert is repeated based on the settings.
Still no reply
An alert goes to the caregiver.

The Result

CareMed helps by:

  • Reminding the patient of medication times.
  • Simplifying use of the app through voice.
  • Tracking the patient's responses.
  • Helping the family see which cases need follow-up.

The core idea:

Reminder→Voice question→Response→Record→Family alert if needed
